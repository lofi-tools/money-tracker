# Portfolio read models and current earnings

Implemented first-UI stage 4 on 12 October 2026. Sources: [query module](../../libs/lib-app/src/portfolio.rs), [exact rational arithmetic](../../libs/lib-app/src/exact.rs), [read transaction](../../libs/lib-core/src/storage.rs), and [worker](../../libs/lib-app/src/worker.rs). Calculation conventions were recorded before implementation; provider population and frontend controls remain separate work.

Queries use one portfolio, a requested cutoff, reporting asset and nonnegative dust threshold. Every position contributes once. Provider observations remain separate from ledger effects. Only complete independent Total/Debt observations at or before the cutoff can supply current quantities; Available/Locked evidence cannot establish total ownership. An account/asset aggregate observation cannot fabricate subscription splits. Different observation timestamps remain visible and never imply verification at a common cutoff.

Current annual earnings hold principal and quotes constant for one year. APY directly multiplies principal; simple APR does too. Explicit APR compounding uses `(1 + APR/n)^n - 1`, for discrete frequencies through 366. Unsupported frequencies remain unknown. Rates are applicable effective held-position annotations, not advertised offers or a switching forecast. Tier/cap/qualification evaluation and unmodelled fees require later source contracts; the query does not invent them. Net earnings subtract evidenced borrowing costs once from gross earnings. Debt uses its positive magnitude; overall current APY divides net annual earnings by gross assets, including idle allocations with an explicit zero rate. Zero gross value has no APY.

Computed financial values are reduced rational numbers with signed numerator and positive denominator encoded as integer strings. Finite decimal amounts, weighted ratios and compounded APR remain exact without binary floats, rounding or an implicit precision limit. Display rounding is later frontend work. Legacy float-price history is never converted to exact input. Quotes for the reporting asset itself are exactly one; all other assets require dated exact evidence or manual prices.

Unknown rate differs from zero. Rollups with some usable contributions return partial known subtotals; with none, they are unknown. Missing borrowing costs make net earnings partial/unknown. Overall APY is withheld unless value and rate coverage are complete. Zero-valued holdings with a nonzero quantity remain visible and still need rate coverage. Historical reward-relative effective APY remains unavailable pending the transaction/reward engine; current immediate earnings must not be labelled inflation-adjusted historical performance.

Active manual aggregate rates cover their whole scope once, preserving pins and review prompts. Child evidence remains in details, and child earnings are withheld when an aggregate cannot be attributed without an invented split. Overlapping manual aggregates resolve pinned first, then update time, then scope specificity and stable identity. Source aggregates apply only where no finer rate evidence exists; partial finer coverage leaves an unknown remainder rather than copying an aggregate rate into it. A missing subscription rate is not inherited from a product offer. Product rates here are held aggregate annotations only.

Dust is per provider/exact asset, strictly below the configured threshold, or manually Dust/Significant. Debt is never dust. Unknown prices cannot trigger automatic dust. Asset navigation retains dust amounts; Dust is an overlapping filtered view and must never be added to asset/provider totals. Spam uses the shared exclusion policy. Local names and effective account parents govern presentation without changing identities. Closed subscription metadata is omitted from current grouping; any nonzero remaining ledger holding stays visible with a warning rather than being discarded.

Frontend, live rate/verification population, receipt/underlying exposure conversion and historical graphs remain later work. Until a reviewed exposure model exists, exact receipt positions count once and no synthetic underlying holdings are created.

## Service and transport

`Application::portfolio_projection(PortfolioRequest)` reads metadata, ledger, independent observations, verification, exact quotes, rates and local policy inside one DuckDB transaction. It returns version 1 with the requested portfolio/cutoff/reporting asset/threshold, persisted revisions, overview, asset/provider navigation, overlapping Dust summary, holdings, rate contributions and allocation nodes. Reads do not change financial records, imports, rates, local actions or revisions. The serialized worker supplies its selected portfolio and saved reporting-currency/dust settings through `portfolio_projection(cutoff)`.

`overview`, `navigation(request, by_asset, search)`, `allocation_tree(request, selection)`, `details(request, target)` and `issues(request, selection)` are convenience services. Consumers can also derive allocation selections and details from one returned projection to retain the same snapshot. Search is case-insensitive name/ticker filtering; neither search nor selection changes overview. Navigation sorts known current values/subtotals descending with deterministic ties, unknown values last. Overview remains a separate summary rather than an entry whose value could be added to the list.

Each `CurrentHolding` retains stable identity, exact quantity/scale, role, ledger quantities alongside independent quantity evidence, original position IDs, subscription/term metadata, selected quote/rate and provenance, dated verification with current/stale flag, dust state, summary and issues. Liability values/costs are positive magnitudes in their dedicated fields; quantities keep ledger signs and net summaries subtract costs/debt once. An asset's borrowing-cost field and a debt's gross-yield field are not applicable. An observation-only aggregate leaf has stable account/asset/role identity; refreshing the observation does not change it.

Allocation nodes form provider → effective account hierarchy → held product within account → subscription → holding. Accounts can have direct holdings as well as children. Ancestor holdings are unique sets; summaries never sum an already-inclusive child twice. A selection filters existing leaves and rebuilds ancestor rollups. Details retain local names, original account names/references, selected evidence IDs, rates/pins, immutable report issues with exact expected/actual quantities, and available lock/maturity/renewal state. No underlying DeFi exposures are synthesized or joined by ticker.

`FigureDto` distinguishes known/partial/unknown/not-applicable. `CalculatedMoney.amount` and current APY use `ExactRatio` with reduced `numerator`/`denominator` integer strings, including terminating decimal inputs; this extends the existing exact decimal DTOs without changing ledger transport. Ratios reject numeric JSON, invalid integers and nonpositive denominators; input representation has an 8-million-character bound per integer. Internal BigInt calculations never truncate to that bound or round. For example, the exact annual fraction 1/3 is `{"numerator":"1","denominator":"3"}`. UI formatting must deliberately round only when presenting it.

Every standalone summary includes `calculation_basis`: the annual estimate is immediate current yield, net of evidenced borrowing only. Unmodelled fees, qualification/tier/cap adjustments and reward retention effects are not claimed as included. A different reward denomination is retained in selected rate metadata; immediate yield assumes conversion at receipt. The separate historical-effective-APY figure is unknown, with its reason. The eventual frontend must retain these qualifications rather than label this as completed historical performance or all-in opportunity comparison.

## Evidence, limitations and conflict handling

Current observation selection uses complete independent Total/Debt records with valid economic signs, choosing freshest cutoff/update/ID deterministically. A newer account total takes precedence over older position observations and remains aggregate when several ledger positions exist. If newer position evidence exists, an older aggregate is not split or added to it; uncovered positions retain explicitly labelled ledger replay. Available/Locked, incomplete, future and non-independent observations never replace total ownership. A single registered bucket can use an account total without losing its subscription identity. Registered positions with no prior effects can use independent evidence; excluded assets remain excluded and external counterparties remain outside the economic perimeter.

Ledger-only quantities remain explicitly unverified rather than silently treated as independently trusted. A mismatch without usable independent evidence withholds affected current values/earnings; row-scoped mismatches do not withhold unrelated rows. Trusted independent balances remain visible despite history mismatch, with dated warnings and retained raw ledger quantities. Historical completeness requires an explicit complete-history verification at the requested cutoff with current ledger/policy revisions. Dated or stale reports retain their evidence and warnings. Source quote timestamps are exposed, not advertised as a common independently verified portfolio cutoff.

An aggregate manual override remains active pending review, including partial new child data. Pinned overrides take precedence; otherwise overlapping manual scopes resolve latest update, specificity (borrowing/subscription, account/product, provider/asset, asset, provider), then stable scope ID. A competing aggregate is not applied to just its uncovered remainder. Source aggregates are used only in the absence of finer evidence. Complete aggregate earnings can be known at the whole scope while child/subset earnings remain unknown; this prevents invented attribution. Actual product summary rates derive from subscriptions unless an explicit held-aggregate annotation applies.

This stage adds no migrations and does not refetch providers. Existing adapters do not yet populate generic exact quote/rate/verification records; old cached provider data therefore returns ledger-derived holdings with honest missing-rate/quote/coverage warnings. Existing strict provider reconciliation stays intact. Reporting assets other than USD work when direct exact quotes are available; no guessed ticker equivalence or automatic FX path is supplied. Receipt/share-underlying valuation, source rate freshness policies and full fee/tier/cap contracts remain outstanding.

Persisted ledger/policy/metadata revisions accompany each snapshot but are not a universal cache key: global source prices/displays and independent observations do not all advance those counters. No projection cache is implemented; consumers must requery after relevant source/local updates, not assume equal counters imply an unchanged result.

## Validation and frontend example

[Synthetic wire fixture](fixtures/current-portfolio.json) supplies version, request context, reporting asset, summary and exact ETH quantity for frontend work. It comes from the deterministic 50% at 5%, 25% at 6%, 25% explicitly idle example: USD 1,000 gross/current value, USD 40 annual earnings and exactly 4% current APY. The test compares actual query transport against this fixture. It contains no private provider data. Intentional contract changes can regenerate it with `UPDATE_PORTFOLIO_FIXTURE=1 cargo test -p lib-app --test portfolio weighted_subscriptions`.

Query tests cover exact values beyond JavaScript/64-bit ranges and tiny quotes, non-terminating ratio JSON, compounded/simple current rates, later effective periods, differing same-template subscriptions, debt signs/costs, partial and wholly unknown figures, known-zero valuation, empty/zero-gross APY, $1,000 + $0.50 dust totals, manual classification, search and portfolio isolation, spam/audit preservation, direct-plus-child account allocation, local/original names, closed/nonzero and zero subscriptions, aggregate pins/review/attribution, independent/mismatched/stale/incomplete/dated evidence, and read failure recovery. Explicit source-withheld current figures are honored even when other rate/price inputs are known. Worker tests verify saved settings/portfolio binding. Provider synthetic mapping/replay/reconciliation tests remain in the deterministic workspace regression suite.

Validation passed: 162 deterministic production-workspace tests, including the final 12-test query suite and managed-worker settings test. Private/live acquisition stayed excluded: 13 opt-in tests ignored and the cache-first Binance test filtered. The separate opt-in offline preservation test passed on temporary copies of all three cached Nexo/Binance databases. Formatting, locked offline Cargo metadata, CLI help, diff checks and changed-document relative links passed. No hosted CI or frontend build is claimed.

## Display APY estimate refinement

The later navbar review adds `estimated_current_apy` alongside strict `current_apy`. The strict figure and individual earnings/rates retain their unknown states. When gross valuation is complete and borrowing costs are complete, an aggregate with only missing investment-rate evidence can display known earnings divided by gross assets, assuming the missing investment rates are zero. Even an entirely unknown investment-rate set can produce a partial 0% estimate under those conditions. This estimate is `partial`, with `unknown_rate_assumed_zero` provenance; the UI marks it `(est.)` and explains the assumption in details. Missing prices, source-withheld APY, unattributable aggregate earnings, unsupported compounding and unknown borrowing costs are not replaced with zero. Historical calculations and strict reconciliation are unchanged. This explicitly refines the earlier interview's aggregate display requirement without manufacturing a known individual rate.
